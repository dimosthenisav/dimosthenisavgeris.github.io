*If I had to write about everything we built in 2024, this post would be way too long. So I made slides instead!*

2024 was all about making leasing easier, more flexible, and more user-friendly. We introduced new verticals, improved the app experience, and gave users more control over their subscriptions.

Rather than writing a long post, here's a **visual recap of everything we delivered** — plus a sneak peek at what's coming next. 🚀

⚠️ **A quick note:** this recap focuses on **user-facing updates**. While we made significant advancements in our internal fleet management tool, instafleet, I can't share those details here. But trust me, **a lot happened behind the scenes too!**
