Outworking everyone is the best method for personal growth within any company.

As I was discussing this recently with a group of friends — H. Takas, K. Giamalis, D. Kalaitzis — we all concluded on the same principle:

> Outworking is not about competition. It's about the discipline of outworking yourself.

Contrary to common belief, "outworking" is not synonymous with working long hours. It's closely tied to the concepts of deep work and consistency.

It's you vs. your past self, and a constant movement towards growth.
